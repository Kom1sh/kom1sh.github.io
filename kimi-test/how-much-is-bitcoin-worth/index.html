What "worth" actually means here

A stock's worth can be anchored to earnings; a bond's to coupon payments. Bitcoin has neither, so its dollar value is purely the meeting point of supply and demand — 19.93 million issued coins against everyone who wants to hold them, from individuals to treasury companies and ETFs. That makes the price volatile by design: bitcoin has halved in value within months more than once, and has also multiplied tenfold in under two years. The current quote sits about 9% below the October 2025 all-time high near $126,000, per our price history.

Frequently asked questions

How much is 1 bitcoin to USD right now?

The figure in this page's headline is the live index quote, refreshed every 60 seconds. It may differ from a specific exchange's last trade by a fraction of a percent.

Why did bitcoin get so expensive?

Fixed supply meeting growing demand. Only 21 million coins will ever exist, issuance halves every four years, and since 2024 US spot ETFs and corporate treasuries have competed for a shrinking float.

Can bitcoin's value go to zero?

In principle, yes — any asset priced purely by demand can lose that demand. Seventeen years of continuous operation and deepening institutional ownership make the scenario less likely over time, but it has never been impossible, and position sizing should reflect that.
