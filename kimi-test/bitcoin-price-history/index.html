Bitcoin price history: every cycle, dated and priced.

When did bitcoin start? January 3, 2009, at a price of exactly zero. What followed were four boom-and-bust cycles, each ending higher than the last — and an all-time high near $126,000 in October 2025.

The full timeline

Jan 3, 2009

The genesis block

Satoshi Nakamoto mines block 0, embedding a newspaper headline about bank bailouts: "The Times 03/Jan/2009 Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks." The 50-BTC reward is unspendable by design. Price: $0 — no market exists yet.

May 22, 2010

First real-world price: $0.0025

Laszlo Hanyecz pays 10,000 BTC for two pizzas — the first documented exchange of bitcoin for goods. At the October 2025 all-time high, those coins were worth about $1.26 billion.

Nov 28, 2012

First halving: 50 → 25 BTC

Block 210,000 cuts the block subsidy in half for the first time. Bitcoin is trading near $12. Thirteen months later it touches $1,100 on Mt. Gox before the exchange's collapse drags the market into a two-year winter.

Dec 17, 2017

The retail mania top: ~$19,700

ICO fever and CME futures launch carry bitcoin to just under $20,000. Twelve months later it trades at $3,200 — an 84% drawdown that convinced most observers the experiment was over. It was not.

May 11, 2020

Third halving amid a pandemic

Block 630,000 cuts issuance to 6.25 BTC two months after COVID crashed the price to ~$4,000. Unprecedented fiscal and monetary stimulus follows, and bitcoin becomes the institutional macro trade of 2020–21.

Nov 10, 2021

~$69,000 and the leverage unwind

Bitcoin peaks near $69,000 as inflation surges. The 2022 unwind — Terra/Luna, Celsius, FTX — takes the price to $15,500 and the industry's leverage to zero. Spot buyers who held through it were made whole within 26 months.

Jan 10, 2024

Spot ETFs approved in the US

The SEC approves eleven spot bitcoin ETFs, including BlackRock's IBIT. It becomes the most successful ETF launch in history, reaching $10 billion in assets in seven weeks. Price breaks the 2021 high in March, touching ~$73,000 ahead of the halving.

Apr 20, 2024

Fourth halving: 3.125 BTC per block

Block 840,000 cuts new issuance to roughly 450 coins per day — less than a third of what US ETFs were absorbing on an average day. The arithmetic of the next eighteen months is set.

Dec 5, 2024

Six figures: $100,000

Five weeks after the US presidential election, bitcoin trades above $100,000 for the first time, peaking near $108,000 mid-month. An asset dismissed as a Ponzi at $100 now clears six figures.

Oct 6, 2025

The all-time high: ~$126,000

ETF inflows, corporate treasury adoption and a softer dollar carry bitcoin to its current record near $126,000. The 2026 consolidation between $95K and $130K is documented on our live chart page.

What the pattern does — and doesn't — tell you

Four cycles share a shape: a halving cuts supply, a mania follows 12–18 months later, an 80% drawdown purges the leverage, and the next cycle bottoms above the last one's top. The 2024–2026 cycle has broken that shape so far — drawdowns have been shallower (31–33% instead of 80%) and the peak came earlier, which some attribute to ETFs smoothing both the upside and the downside. Whether that dampening persists is the open question of this cycle. What history does not tell you is what happens next; the risks section of our buying guide covers the honest caveats.
